# Pak Rummy Registration Guide

Pak Rummy registration is simple for Android users.

https://pakrummy.app

## Registration Process

1. Open Pak Rummy App
2. Tap Register
3. Enter your information
4. Create account credentials
5. Verify account

## Requirements

- Android smartphone
- Internet connection
- Mobile number

- Account access

- Secure login system
